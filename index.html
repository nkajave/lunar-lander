Learning curve

Test flights, game score Practice, score the AI is chasing

Test flights use the AI's best move every frame and the game's own score, with no bonus. Above the green line counts as solved. Practice flights include some random moves so it can explore, and count your bonus.

How the AI learns

It sees eight numbers

Position, speed, tilt, spin and whether each leg touches the ground. That's the panel under the game.

It picks one of four moves

Do nothing, fire the left engine, the main engine, or the right engine. Fifty times a second.

It chases points

A small neural network tries moves, sees which ones earned more points, and nudges itself toward them. The method is PPO, the same one in the Python code.

Why one of them cheats

An attempt ends after 1,000 frames. Pay +1 for every steady frame and a rocket hanging in the air can collect up to 1,000 points, while landing ends the game and stops the payments. So hovering beats landing, and the AI finds that out. It learns exactly what it's paid for, not what you meant. The fix pays only for becoming steady, so the bonus adds up to at most 1 per attempt and there's nothing left to farm.
